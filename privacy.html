Privacy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

MCOC Wordle is a free fan-made word game. There are no accounts or sign-ups, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail.

Your game data stays on your device. Your daily progress, stats and streaks are saved in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us. Clearing this site's data deletes them.

Who receives data. The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes requests (including IP addresses) to deliver the page. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them. There are no analytics, ads, cookies or tracking, and nothing is sold.

This game is not affiliated with Kabam or Marvel.
